City Performance · Big Ambitions mod

Five experiments you control.

Each one sits under Settings > Mods > City Performance and starts on. If problems occur, enable logging and disable features one by one.

OnSmooth pedestrian refillsSpreads a pedestrian refill over a few frames.
OnShare stationary-zone spawn budgetFewer street performers and standing characters appear in one frame.
OnDistance-based tree detailDistant trees use their lighter mesh and stay visible.
OnBatch midnight asset cleanupsThe midnight memory cleanup runs less often, behind a loading screen where possible.
OnBackground autosavesWritten on a background thread, after the first one is checked byte for byte. The riskiest option: keep a manual save.
